How the position/time CSV was produced

1. VideoOriginal 1920×1080 recording at 120 frames/s.
2. BackgroundTemporal-median background built from 41 frames.
3. DetectionBright-particle peak found independently in front and side camera panels.
4. LinkingNearest plausible detection followed frame-to-frame; jumps above 0.15 mm rejected.
5. CalibrationFront horizontal gives x, side horizontal gives y, and both give z.
6. SmoothingRaw XYZ retained; separate Savitzky–Golay smoothed columns added.
7. Modes1–4 Hz XY band, 0.5 s RMS envelope, and threshold sensitivity identify circular bursts.

What the columns mean

  • x_mm, y_mm, z_mm: calibrated measurements retained without smoothing.
  • x_smooth_mm, y_smooth_mm, z_smooth_mm: trajectory used for mode analysis.
  • orbit_band_*: 1–4 Hz component used to isolate the fast orbit.
  • orbit_rms_mm: 0.5 s XY RMS envelope.
  • circular_episode: primary 0.10 mm threshold classification.
  • bottom_temperature_c: 1 Hz graph trace interpolated to video time.

Uncertainty and error bands

    Interpretation

    Random precision is estimated from robust raw-minus-smoothed residuals. It measures short-term tracking scatter, not full calibration accuracy.

    Episode variability is the standard deviation across the seven orbit bursts and includes real physical changes as well as measurement noise.

    Boundary uncertainty comes from rerunning the detector at 0.08, 0.10, and 0.15 mm RMS thresholds. It is shown in amber.

    Systematic error remains in the absolute position scale because the original SP6 calibration is unavailable. It cannot honestly be converted into a finite confidence interval from this video alone.
